## Design decisions

- One explicit conceptual intervention per built-in candidate.
- Structured diagnosis constrains candidate categories.
- Validation evidence is adaptive; sealed research-OOS is bounded and post-shortlist only.
- Research does not mutate model-pool selection or external deployment state.

## Known limitations

- Formal FDR/Bayesian multiple-testing correction is not implemented.
- Bayesian HPO, neural architecture search, arbitrary feature synthesis, and deployment remain out of scope.
- Some intervention kinds are reserved for extension but do not yet have built-in executors.
