## Behaviour added or corrected

- Datasets no longer need datetime columns; ordered IDs, stable hashes/groups, predefined splits, and temporal splits share one partition contract.
- Recalibration fits only on fit data, must improve validation, and touches refresh-OOS only after that freeze decision.
- Retraining creates a new immutable model identity and similarly reaches refresh-OOS only after validation improvement.
- Retention uses refresh scorecards, minimum sample gates, optional validation-to-test degradation limits, and compatible objective/metric scope.
- Pool snapshots and refresh reports are immutable per iteration.

## Design decisions

- Partition semantics are explicit and dataset-agnostic rather than timestamp-assumed.
- Refresh-OOS is recurring operational evidence, distinct from sealed research OOS.
- Retraining and recalibration create new identities/artifacts rather than mutating historical fitted state.
- Refresh operates known candidates; open-ended automated candidate generation remains MILESTONE-0011.

## Known limitations

- Contextual selection, diversity-aware retention, feature-distribution drift detection, and automatic research candidate generation are not implemented yet.
- Built-in calibration currently provides a simple affine reference implementation.
