## Design decisions

- Model identity, calibration, benchmark history, and lifecycle events remain separate records.
- Local filesystem storage is the default, while registry and serialization are replaceable protocols.
- Objective compatibility is required for ordinary model comparison.
- Lifecycle state is independent from ranking and deployment.

## Known limitations

- Automatic drift detection, recalibration policy, top-K model pools, and contextual selection are intentionally deferred to MILESTONE-0010.
- Remote registry backends are not bundled.
