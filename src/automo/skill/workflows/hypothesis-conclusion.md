# Hypothesis conclusion

Conclude an active hypothesis when evidence is sufficient at its committed evaluation depth, the budget is exhausted, or a blocker makes further evidence collection non-decision-useful.

1. Preserve all prior plans, candidates, exposures, and evidence.
2. Evaluate required local/parent/system objectives rather than relying on one convenient metric.
3. Record limitations and contradictory evidence.
4. Conclude as `supported`, `rejected`, or `inconclusive`.
5. Do not rewrite a rejected child hypothesis as successful merely because a local metric improved when required parent/system contribution failed.
6. Return project implications to GetDone/project planning as recommendations, not as an Automo milestone transition.
