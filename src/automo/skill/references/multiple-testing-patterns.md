# Multiple-testing patterns

## Narrow pre-specified comparison
One baseline versus one candidate on a committed metric and split has low selection pressure. Normal validation evidence may be sufficient before the planned OOS gate.

## Small bounded search
Several candidates generated by a fixed rule require complete attempt accounting and a stronger validation margin. Prefer consistency across folds/periods rather than a single best score.

## Adaptive search
When later candidates depend on observed earlier results, treat the sequence as adaptive research. Preserve all attempts, limit iterations, and require a fresh plan boundary when the conceptual intervention changes.

## Repeated OOS inspection
Repeatedly choosing based on sealed OOS converts OOS into validation. Do not do this. Consume only the precommitted OOS slots and create a genuinely new evidence boundary for later research.
