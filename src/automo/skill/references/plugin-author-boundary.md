# Consumer Plugin Boundary

Automo owns research governance, evidence/OOS safety, registry lifecycle, refresh orchestration, guidance selection, and `.automo/` state. A consuming domain package should provide thin data sources, features, objectives, evaluators, trainers/predictors, split strategies, selectors, calibrators, services, and optional `.project-agent/automo/` guidance. Do not duplicate Automo milestone, registry, sealed-OOS, or research-budget machinery in the consumer merely to integrate.
